# Soren Dayton

I direct the American Governance program at the [Foundation for American Innovation](https://www.thefai.org), working on how Congress, the administrative state, and the AI transition fit together. Before that: about two decades in governance, communications, and policy, and a stretch as a PhD student in anthropology and linguistics at the University of Chicago, which still shapes how I read institutions.

This account holds public data and tools from that work. Everything here is open-source research, maintained by me, and not validated against primary records. Treat it as a starting point and send corrections.

## Here

- **[120th House Freshman Tracker](https://sorendayton.github.io/house-freshman-tracker/)** — every new member-elect of the U.S. House, with prior legislative service, committee assignments, leadership roles, and predicted caucus memberships. Updated as races are called and caucuses announce. [Repository and data files](https://github.com/sorendayton/house-freshman-tracker).
